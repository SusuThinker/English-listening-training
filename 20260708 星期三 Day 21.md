# 三、美国小费文化

## 一、中文介绍

### 1\. 底层逻辑

在美国服务业，服务员、调酒师、送餐员、保洁等岗位**基础时薪极低**，收入主要依靠客人小费，小费被视作员工正常薪资的一部分，不是可选的额外奖赏，绝大多数场景必须支付。

拒绝合理小费会被认为极度不礼貌，服务差可减少小费，但完全不给容易引发冲突。



### 2\. 通用小费比例（税前账单金额计算）

1. 餐厅堂食（正餐服务员）

    - 普通服务：15%

    - 良好服务：18%

    - 优质贴心服务：20%及以上

2. 快餐、自助餐厅：无需小费，自愿给1–3美元放小费罐即可

3. 酒吧调酒师：每杯酒1–2美元，或总账单15%–20%

4. 外卖送餐员：15%–20%，最低3–5美元；雨雪天建议多加

5. 酒店服务

    - 行李员：每件行李1–2美元

    - 客房保洁：每晚2–5美元，放床头并附留言

    - 门童帮忙叫车：1–5美元

6. 美发、美甲、按摩：15%–20%

7. 出租车/网约车司机：15%–20%

8. 导游：每日5–10美元/人

### 3\. 重要规则

1. 账单若标注「Gratuity / Service Charge（服务费）」，代表店家已自动收取18%–20%小费，无需额外再给；

2. 多人聚餐、大型派对，商家常自动加收服务费；

3. 小费以现金最优，刷卡小费会延后到员工手中，现金直接归服务人员；

4. 小费计算基数是**税前金额**，不含税费。

## 二、English Introduction

## Guide to Tipping Culture in the US \& Standard Tip Percentages

### Core Concept

Service industry workers in the U\.S\. receive a very low base hourly wage\. Tips make up the majority of their income, so tipping is treated as mandatory compensation rather than an optional bonus\. Refusing to tip appropriately is widely seen as rude\. You may lower the tip for terrible service, but skipping it entirely often causes unpleasant disputes\.



### Standard Tip Rates \(Calculated on pre\-tax bills\)

1. Full\-service restaurant servers

    - Average service: 15%

    - Good service: 18%

    - Excellent service: 20% or higher

2. Fast food \& buffet restaurants: No mandatory tip; $1–3 voluntary cash for tip jars

3. Bartenders: $1–2 per drink, or 15%–20% of the total tab

4. Food delivery drivers: 15%–20% \(minimum $3–5\); extra tips recommended on rainy/snowy days

5. Hotel staff

    - Bellhops: $1–2 per bag

    - Housekeeping: $2–5 per night, leave cash on the pillow with a short note

    - Doormen who hail cabs: $1–5

6. Hair stylists, nail technicians, massage therapists: 15%–20%

7. Taxi \& rideshare drivers: 15%–20%

8. Tour guides: $5–10 per person per day

### Key Rules

1. If the bill states "Gratuity" or "Service Charge", an automatic 18%–20% tip has been added by the restaurant, so no extra tip is needed\.

2. Large group dining usually comes with an automatic service fee\.

3. Cash tips are preferred\. Tips added to card payments take time to reach staff, while cash goes directly to servers\.

4. Tips are calculated based on the subtotal before tax, excluding sales tax\.
