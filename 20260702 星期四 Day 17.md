“*The company has been **in the black** for the past six months\.*“

“*Most of the employees **see eye\-to\-eye** on the future of the company*\.”

5. **Culture 1: Answering Machine 欧美电话答录机文化**
In Western countries, answering machines were widely used before smartphones\. The classic fixed voice prompt: **Please leave a message after the beep\.** 哔声后请留言，是听力超高频经典句式，常用于日常口语、影视及听力素材场景。

6. **Culture 2: Western Formal Wear Culture 欧美礼服穿搭文化**
Western people seldom buy expensive formal suits or tuxedos for rare occasions\. They prefer **rent formal dresses and tuxedos** 租赁礼服，which is more economical\. Meanwhile, they advocate personalized dressing and unique tie matching, refusing rigid and unified wearing styles\.

8. **Culture 4: Hippopotamus Etymology 河马趣味词源**
**hippopotamus /ˌhɪpəˈpɒtəməs/ n\.** 河马
Etymology origin: Greek compound word\. **hippo** means horse, **potamos** means river\. The literal meaning is "horse of the river" 河里的马，是英语经典词根合成趣味词汇。
